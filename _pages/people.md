* [UCL Birkbeck MRC Doctoral Training Programme](https://www.uclbbk-mrcdtp.ac.uk/)
* [UCL BSc/MSci Biochemistry](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/life-sciences/study/undergraduate/biochemistry-degrees) programme: join us as a Year 4 Research Project student, or for summer project opportunities
