    • UCL Birkbeck MRC Doctoral Training Programme
    • UCL BSc/MSci Biochemistry programme: join us as a Year 4 Research Project student, or for summer project opportunities
    • Join us for MSc/MRes research projects: e.g. UCL MRes Computational Cell Biophysics, UCL MRes Biosciences, UCL MSc Biomedical Sciences

    Please feel free to email Joseph to discuss possibilities to join the lab.
